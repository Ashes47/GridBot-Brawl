🎮 Game Guide

Learn how to participate, upload your bots, and master the strategic depths of GridBot Brawl.

Overview

GridBot Brawl is a turn-based coding arena. Teams submit one Python file that implements 18 component classes. On signup, you select exactly 5 distinct components as your roster. Matches run on a 10×10 grid (duo) or 15×15 grid (quad) and last up to 100 turns.

How to Play

  1. Write a single .py file that defines the 18 classes listed below. Each class implements decide(self, obs) and returns an action dictionary.
  2. Go to Team Signup, enter team info, select 5 components, and upload your file.
  3. Queue matches via the Team page or evaluation; watch replays in the Match Viewer.

Rules

  • All bots start with 100 HP.
  • Movement is orthogonal; some powers allow diagonal placement (walls/decoy) or diagonal jumps (leap).
  • Visibility uses Chebyshev radius 4; LoS is blocked by bots, decoys, walls, and forest.
  • End-of-turn: apply DoT, decrement cooldowns/statuses/TTLs, zones (Boost/Heal/Damage/Teleport), expire shields.
  • No friendly fire exceptions: AoE (bomber/trap) can hit allies.

Maps & Terrain

🗺️ Map System: Every match runs on a seed-generated map defined by maps/map_rules.json. Maps are randomly assigned server-side for fairness. See the Maps Guide for detailed terrain and zone mechanics.

Quick Reference:

Walls: Block movement and line-of-sight
Forest: Blocks LoS, allows movement
Swamp: Slows bots next turn
Ice: Causes sliding movement
Water: Impassable, doesn't block LoS
💚 Heal Zone: +20 HP at end of turn
💀 Damage Zone: -10 HP at end of turn
⚡ Boost Zone: Extra cooldown reduction
✨ Teleport Zone: Random relocation

  "visible_traps": [ [5,5, "a1"] ],
  "visible_terrain": [ [2,3, "forest"], [7,7, "swamp"] ],
  "visible_zones": [ [2,8, ["heal"]], [5,5, ["boost"]] ]
}
  • visible_walls: wall tiles within radius 4 (Chebyshev).
  • visible_static_walls: map walls within radius 4.
  • visible_decoys: decoy positions within radius 4 as [x,y, team_id].
  • visible_traps: only allied traps (team visibility) within radius 4 as [x,y, team_id].
  • visible_terrain: terrain tiles within radius 4 as [x,y, type].
  • visible_zones: zones within radius 4 as [x,y, [types...]].

Ranking & Leaderboard (Player Guide)

  

    We use a proven system called TrueSkill to rank teams. Think of it as:
    

    Skill (how strong your team has shown to be) and Uncertainty (how sure we are).
    Your visible rank uses a safety‑first Final Score = Skill − 3 × Uncertainty.
  

  
    
    
  • New teams start at Skill 25 with some Uncertainty. Win matches to increase Skill and reduce Uncertainty.
    • 
    
  • Final Score goes up faster when you beat strong opponents, and settles as Uncertainty drops.
    • 
    
  • Duo vs Quad: You have separate ratings for each mode.
    • 
    
  • Quad placements: Survivors outrank eliminated; ties use total HP, then total damage. Remaining ties are broken consistently.
    • 
    
  • Baselines: We use three fixed teams (A/B/C) to calibrate newcomers and verify drift. They don’t crowd the public board.
    • 
    
  • Recent form: See your last 10 results (W/L) and your W‑L over the last 50.
    • 
  

  

    Clicking Submit for Evaluation schedules a short calibration set against baselines and similarly rated teams.
    After that, our scheduler keeps your rating fresh with daily matchups—mostly near your rank, with some exploration.
