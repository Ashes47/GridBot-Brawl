🗺️ Maps, Terrain & Zones

Master the dynamic battlefield with procedurally generated maps featuring diverse terrain types and strategic zones. Every match presents unique tactical challenges!

🎲 Map Generation: All maps are server-generated using seeds for fairness. Each match features a unique map_name and map_seed for deterministic replays. Maps scale from 10×10 (duo) to 15×15 (quad) with balanced spawn positions.

Terrain Types

Terrain affects movement, line-of-sight, and tactical positioning. Each tile can have at most one terrain type.

Static Walls
Permanent barriers that shape the battlefield layout and create chokepoints.
Movement: Completely impassable - blocks all movement including Dash, Leap, and teleportation landing.
Line of Sight: Blocks all LoS for attacks, powers, and vision.
Strategy: Use for cover, funnel enemies into kill zones, protect backline positioning. Wall Builders can create temporary versions.
Forest
Dense woodland that provides concealment while allowing movement through.
Movement: Free passage - no penalties or restrictions.
Line of Sight: Blocks LoS including diagonal corner-cutting. Cannot snipe through forest corners.
Strategy: Break enemy sightlines, counter snipers, create ambush positions. Scout mobility shines in forest terrain.
Swamp
Treacherous wetlands that bog down movement for the following turn.
Movement: Entry allowed, but ending turn on swamp applies Slowed status.
Slowed Effect: Next turn cannot use Move, Dash, or Leap actions. Powers still work.
Tactical Warning: Dash and Leap fail if ANY traversed tile is swamp. Plan movement carefully!
Strategy: Area denial, punish aggressive positioning. Puller can force enemies into swamp. Avoid when escaping.
Ice
Slippery surfaces that cause normal movement to slide additional tiles.
Movement: Normal Move onto ice slides up to 2 extra tiles in the same direction.
Special Rules: Dash and Leap do NOT trigger sliding. Sliding stops at blockers.
Strategy: Unexpected positioning, surprise flanks, or accidental overextension. Can slide into danger or safety - plan carefully!
Water
Deep water that blocks all movement while maintaining clear sightlines.
Movement: Completely impassable - blocks Move, Dash, Leap, and teleportation.
Line of Sight: Transparent - does NOT block LoS. Snipers can shoot over water.
Tactical Warning: Yank and Shove cannot place targets in water - abilities fail if destination is water.
Strategy: Natural barriers that channel movement while preserving sightlines. Use for kiting and range advantage.

Zone Effects

Zones activate at end of turn for bots standing on the tile. Multiple zones can stack on the same tile!

💚 Heal Zone
Regenerative energy that restores health to wounded bots.
Effect: +20 HP at end of turn (capped at 100 max HP).
Timing: Applies after damage/poison but before shield expiration.
Strategy: Control heal zones for sustain advantage. Combine with tanks for extended frontline presence. Contest with poison/damage.
💀 Damage Zone
Hazardous areas that harm any bot unfortunate enough to linger.
Effect: -10 HP at end of turn.
Shield Interaction: Damage IS reduced by active shields (75% reduction).
Strategy: Area denial, finish wounded enemies. Push/Pull enemies onto damage zones. Shield Givers can mitigate damage.
⚡ Boost Zone
Energizing fields that accelerate power recovery beyond normal rates.
Effect: After normal cooldown decrement, reduce ALL cooldowns by 1 additional turn (minimum 0).
Stacking: Multiple boost zones on same tile don't stack.
Strategy: Power spam, faster combo setups. Especially valuable for high-cooldown powers like Snipe, Explode, Teleport.
✨ Teleport Zone
Unstable spatial rifts that relocate bots to random battlefield positions.
Effect: Instantly teleport to a random empty tile anywhere on the map.
Valid Destinations: Any empty tile except walls, static walls, water, or occupied spaces.
Unpredictable: Can teleport into enemy territory, safety, or isolated positions. High risk, high reward!
Strategy: Escape mechanism, surprise flanks, or disruption. Risk/reward varies greatly by game state.

Advanced Mechanics

🎯 Line of Sight Rules

  • Blockers: Static walls, player walls, forest, bots, and decoys block LoS
  • Transparent: Water, all zone types, swamp, and ice do NOT block LoS
  • Forest Corner Rule: Cannot "corner cut" diagonally through forest tiles
  • Range Limits: Snipe (5 tiles), most powers (3 tiles), visibility (4 Chebyshev)

⚡ End of Turn Processing Order

  1. Poison/trap/wall TTLs decrement and expire
  2. Normal power cooldowns decrement
  3. Boost zones provide extra cooldown reduction
  4. Heal zones restore HP (capped at 100)
  5. Damage zones deal damage (reduced by shields)
  6. Teleport zones relocate bots
  7. Swamp applies Slowed status for next turn
  8. Shields expire after their duration

🛡️ Terrain Interactions

  • Power Failures: Dash/Leap fail if ANY traversed tile is swamp or water
  • Displacement Limits: Yank/Shove fail if destination is water
  • Teleportation: Random teleports avoid walls, water, and occupied tiles
  • Ice Sliding: Only normal Move triggers sliding; powers don't slide
  • Zone Stacking: Multiple zones on same tile all apply (rare but possible)

Strategic Map Reading

🧠 Pro Tips: Study the map before committing to positioning. Identify chokepoints, LoS lanes, escape routes, and zone control opportunities. Adapt your team composition mental model to the terrain!
